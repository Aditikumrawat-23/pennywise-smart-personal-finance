# 💰 Pennywise — Smart Personal Finance & Expense Tracking

> A mobile FinTech experience designed to make everyday money tracking simple, visual, and easy to understand.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

**Pennywise** is a Smart Personal Finance & Expense Tracking mobile app designed to help users understand their spending, track expenses, manage their monthly budget, and view financial insights in a simple and visually clear interface.

This project was created as a **UI/UX Design Task — Task 1**.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

People make multiple financial transactions every day using UPI, cards, and digital wallets, but it can be difficult to understand:

- Where their money is being spent
- How much budget is remaining
- Which categories consume the most money
- How their spending changes over time

Pennywise addresses this problem by bringing expense tracking, budgeting, and spending insights into one simple mobile experience.

---

## 💡 Objective

The goal was to design a FinTech mobile application that provides:

- Clear monthly spending information
- Easy expense entry
- Category-wise spending analysis
- Monthly financial insights
- Simple profile and account management

---

## 👥 Target Users

- Students
- Fresh graduates
- Working professionals
- Regular digital payment users
- People who want a simple way to track everyday expenses
